| 1 | Camera darts: auto-commit or host confirmation? | Auto-commit after ~3 seconds with no correction; one tap fixes it. |
| 2 | Mounting hardware and power | Ceiling mount: 7.5 ft basement ceiling, bull at 5 ft 8 in, so the phone is about 52 cm above the bull. The owner proposed 0.5 ft from the wall; that is a ~16° grazing view, and a dart in the bull would hide about 1.2 m of board below it. Recommended: 1.5–2 ft from the wall (41–49° view, ~9–14 cm hidden below each dart), via a drop arm or by moving the mount, with no obstacles. **Owner will mount at 1.5 ft (~41°).** Usable range is about 1.5–3 ft (41–60°); beyond ~60° the camera faces the board and each dart starts hiding its own tip. Add "ceiling-0.5ft", "ceiling-1.5ft" and "ceiling-2ft" mounts to the synthetic harness. Power cable to the phone. |
