Connect phone

Do this now

  1. On the phone, open IP Webcam, scroll to the bottom and tap Start server.
  2. The phone shows an address such as http://192.168.1.23:8080. Type the numbers below.
  3. Press Test connection, then Connect. The picture appears on the right.

No camera connected.

Can't connect? Things to check
  • The phone and this Mac are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network).
  • IP Webcam is open on the phone and shows Start server was pressed (the camera picture is on the phone screen).
  • The numbers match the phone exactly, for example 192.168.1.23. They can change after the phone restarts.
  • The phone screen is on and the phone is plugged in. A locked phone stops sending pictures.
  • Still stuck: choose IP Webcam · single photos as the App, or try DroidCam.
  • To rehearse without a phone, run .venv/bin/python tests/fake_ipwebcam.py --port 8080 and connect to 127.0.0.1.
Phone settings checklist (IP Webcam)
  • Main 1× rear camera (not the 0.6× ultrawide).
  • Video resolution 1920×1080, or 1280×720 if the stream is slower than 12 fps.
  • Quality about 80, FPS limit 15.
  • Focus locked (fixed focus at the board), exposure and white balance locked after framing.
  • Video stabilisation off.
  • Keep the screen awake (IP Webcam: prevent the phone from sleeping) and exempt the app from battery optimisation.
  • Phone plugged in to power.
  • LED flicker or bands in the picture: set anti-banding to 60 Hz.
2

Calibrate

After the phone is connected

  1. Take all darts out of the board.
  2. Press Freeze and set four points.
  3. On the picture, click the outer edge of the double ring at the middle of 20 (top), 6 (right), 3 (bottom), then 11 (left). A magnifier follows the cursor; small misses are corrected for you.
  4. Check the drawn rings sit on the wires, then press Markers correct · Ready to play.

Mount the phone securely with the whole board in view and 20 at the top.

If the phone or board moves, press Check / realign board and check the rings again. Board clear · reset image tells the camera the board is empty now (use it after pulling darts if it gets confused).
