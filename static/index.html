In the IP Webcam app, scroll to the bottom and tap Start server. The phone shows its address, for example http://192.168.1.23:8080. Type the numbers here. Phone and Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Live view

Waiting for the camera

Mount the phone securely with the whole board in view and 20 at the top.

Click the OUTER edge of the double ring at the centre of 20 (top), 6 (right), 3 (bottom), then 11 (left). A magnifier follows the cursor. Afterwards every ring and sector line is drawn on the live image: check they sit on the wires before you verify. If the phone or board moves, check alignment and verify again.

Camera position: set four points first.

Only change this if the arrow points the wrong way. It tells the detector which end of a dart is the tip.
Phone settings checklist (IP Webcam)
  • Main 1× rear camera (not the 0.6× ultrawide).
  • Video resolution 1920×1080, or 1280×720 if the stream is slower than 12 fps.
  • Quality about 80, FPS limit 15.
  • Focus locked (fixed focus at the board), exposure and white balance locked after framing.
  • Video stabilisation off.
  • Keep the screen awake (IP Webcam: prevent the phone from sleeping) and exempt the app from battery optimisation.
  • Phone plugged in to power.
  • LED flicker or bands in the picture: set anti-banding to 60 Hz.

Recording for the trial

Not recording.
Records the camera stream and every dart, correction and calibration step, so the trial can be replayed and labelled later. Footage stays on this Mac in the recordings folder and is never uploaded; delete a session folder to remove it. Recording stops by itself when less than 5 GB is free.
